Editorial standard

How AI Tools Lab works

1 · Evidence

Primary sources first

Current pricing, licensing and product facts are checked against vendor documentation and dated.

2 · Tests

Direct use is labeled

Documentation research is not presented as a hands-on benchmark. Real tests publish method and limitations.

3 · Trade-offs

Alternatives matter

Price, setup burden, licensing, portability and better-fit alternatives are part of the decision.

4 · Incentives

Commercial relationships are visible

Affiliate or sponsored relationships are disclosed rather than hidden inside the recommendation.
