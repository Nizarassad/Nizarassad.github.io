AI Tools Lab

Useful AI tools, tested instead of hyped.

A technical publishing project focused on evidence-led reviews, comparisons, tutorials and real demonstrations of AI, developer, productivity and automation software. The goal is simple: help people decide what is actually worth their time or money.

Editorial focus

AI & automation

Agents, research tools, workflow automation and practical AI products tested on real tasks.

Developer tools

Coding assistants, APIs, infrastructure and developer SaaS evaluated from a technical user's perspective.

Productivity

Tools that claim to save time, reduce repetitive work or replace more expensive software.

Comparisons

Side-by-side tests based on the same input, task and constraints rather than feature-list summaries.

How products will be evaluated

  • Use the product. Promotional claims should be backed by direct testing or clearly identified external evidence.
  • Show the result. Screen recordings, reproducible tests and concrete outputs are preferred over generic stock footage.
  • Include trade-offs. Price, limitations and better alternatives matter as much as strengths.
  • Separate attention from value. A trending tool is not automatically a useful tool.
  • Disclose incentives. Affiliate relationships will be identified clearly when links can generate a commission.

Audience

English-speaking developers, data/AI practitioners, freelancers, creators, knowledge workers and small teams looking for practical software recommendations. Initial editorial coverage is global, with particular relevance to products available in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and Europe.
