How it works

Three steps. Keep the first attempt simple.

01

Paste a real script

Open Text to Speech and use a short passage from the project you actually want to publish. Include difficult names or technical terms.

02

Choose a voice

Pick the voice and model before generating. The trial used Roger with Eleven Multilingual v2.

03

Generate and review

Listen while reading your script. Check missing words, pronunciation, pacing, and whether the delivery fits your video.

The actual test

One difficult sentence. Two distinct takes.

The test deliberately used technical language instead of an easy marketing sentence. Generation 2 was preferred for this passage, but two takes are not enough to claim universal quality.

Quick decision

Should you try it?

Good fit if…
  • You want voiceovers without recording yourself.
  • You create videos, ads, podcasts, or tutorials.
  • You value speed and a simple web interface.
  • You want to test a short script before building a full workflow.
Look elsewhere if…
  • You need a fully local or self-hosted voice system.
  • You require zero ongoing subscription cost.
  • You cannot review generated audio before publishing.
  • Your use case falls outside the product’s terms.
